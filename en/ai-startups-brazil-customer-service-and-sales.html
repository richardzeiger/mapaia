Mapa IA Brasil (AI Map Brazil) is a free, open map of Brazilian startups with artificial intelligence at the core of their product. Companies are found through AI-assisted research in news, websites and social media and through applications, and go through a monthly curation by MSW Capital, a Brazilian venture capital firm, with four criteria: being Brazilian, having a product in use, having AI as an essential part of the product and publicly presenting itself as an AI company. The map does not cover every AI company in Brazil. The AI-native badge goes to companies founded in 2022 or later with AI at the core of the product from the start. Startups can apply or request corrections at mapaiabr.com.br; investors, in the VCs in AI tab. The interactive map is in Portuguese. Contact: contato@mapaiabr.com.br. Powered by MSW Capital.
